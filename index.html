A little about us

Good food
feels like
coming home.

SAVOR & SEED began with a simple belief: the best nights are made of generous plates, a bottle passed around, and nowhere else to be.

Our kitchen follows the seasons, our bar follows your mood, and our dining room is built for one more at the table. Come as you are. Stay for dessert.
